Screening

Simple Screening Requirements.

For first-time clients, screening is mandatory to make an appointment, no exceptions. You must send a selfie holding your ID (drivers license or state issued ID) or LinkedIn via text message (preferred method) or email.

When contacting me please include the following:
• Name
• Age
• Ethnicity
• Date of desired appointment
• Time of desired appointment
• Name of the city you are currently in
• Duration of appointment

If you do not provide this basic information, I will not reply with any information.

If the text message isn't replied to, please contact me via email, WeChat, or WhatsApp, as sometimes SMS messages may be restricted or delayed.

Thank you.
