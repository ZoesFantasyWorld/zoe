MINIMAL CLEANING WEBSITE

1. Open index.html in your browser to preview the site.
2. Replace "Your Cleaning Co." with your real business name.
3. Replace the phone, email, service area, hours, rates, reviews, and page text.
4. Put your photos inside the images folder.
5. The Schedule and Contact forms are demo-only right now. JavaScript prevents them
   from actually sending anything and shows a message after submission.
6. The next step is connecting the forms to a real service such as Formspree,
   Netlify Forms, EmailJS, Google Calendar, Calendly, Square Appointments, or another booking system.
