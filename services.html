Simple Screening Requirements.

For first-time clients, screening is mandatory to make an appointment, no exceptions.

A selfie holding your ID or LinkedIn must be sent via text message or email.

When contacting me please include name, age, ethnicity, date, time, the name of the city you are currently in, and duration that you desire.

If you do not provide this basic information, I will not reply with any information.

If the text message isn’t replied to, please contact me via email, WeChat, or WhatsApp, as sometimes SMS messages may be restricted or delayed.
